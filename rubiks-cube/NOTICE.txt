Adapted for 花前月下 in October 2026:
- Split inline styles and scripts into same-origin files for the blog's CSP.
- Applied the blog's fonts, colours, responsive layout and shared theme.
- Added blog navigation, accessible controls and a native formula dialog.
- Fixed the documented R shortcut and limited keyboard capture to cube commands.
- Adjusted camera framing and added reduced-motion support and WebGL error feedback.
- Kept layer turns visible under reduced motion and added saved animation speed controls.
- Rebuilt interaction around exact sticker identities and shared state for 2–7 orders.
- Added continuous pointer turns, simple/advanced modes, contiguous wide turns,
  circular orbit and animated net views, history, independent formula demonstrations,
  and validated browser-local persistence. All 35 existing algorithm examples are retained.
- Circular tracks are derived from actual axis rotations, projected into the plane.
  Each dot represents one sticker; fixed points on an axis are explicitly marked.
- Added a complete 3x3 CFOP directory (41 F2L, 57 OLL, 21 PLL), cross guidance,
  4x4 reduction and parity examples, model-generated case diagrams, Chinese
  instruction text and step-by-step notation explanations. Algorithm references
  and notation conventions are documented in FORMULAS.md.
